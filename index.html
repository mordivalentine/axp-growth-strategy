06 • Tailored Growth Levers

Six Immediate Opportunities for AXP

High-leverage initiatives designed specifically around AXP’s assets, prospective homebuyers, and commercial goals.

Flagship Opportunity

Build a HomeReady™ Acquisition Funnel

Use educational campaigns to attract aspiring homeowners, guide them through readiness content, capture interest, and nurture them smoothly toward relevant next steps.

Organic Engine

Turn the Knowledge Centre into Traffic

Publish search-focused topic clusters answering prospective homebuyers’ real questions on homeownership, mortgages, affordability, financial readiness, and housing opportunities.

Conversion Focus

Dedicated Opportunity Pathways

Give each property or homeownership opportunity its own tailored strategy, audience, landing page, creative, enquiry mechanism, and follow-up pathway.

Paid Media

Paid Ads Beyond Simple Awareness

Deploy Meta and Google campaigns across awareness, traffic, lead generation, retargeting, and conversion—measuring lead quality and progression, not reach alone.

Owned Asset

Build Email as a Business Asset

Capture interest through resources, newsletters, and enquiries. Segment contacts and nurture them continuously to reduce long-term reliance on paid ads.

Alignment

Sales-to-Marketing Feedback Loop

Partner closely with sales and business development to assess lead quality, conversion rates, reasons for non-conversion, and optimisation opportunities.
